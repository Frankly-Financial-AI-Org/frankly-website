Data Handling, Confidentiality & Use of Technology Policy.

Frankly Financial Ltd. · Last updated October 26, 2026

1Purpose and Scope

This Policy describes how Frankly Financial Ltd. (“Frankly,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, protects, and retains information provided by clients and prospective clients (“you”) in connection with our bookkeeping, tax, and advisory services (the “Services”). This Policy forms part of, and is incorporated by reference into, our engagement agreements. In the event of a conflict between this Policy and a signed engagement agreement, the engagement agreement governs.

2Confidentiality

All information you provide to us — including financial statements, banking records, tax records, payroll information, and business documents (“Client Information”) — is treated as confidential. We use Client Information solely for the purpose of delivering the Services, meeting our professional obligations, and complying with applicable law. We do not sell Client Information, and we do not disclose it to any third party except as described in this Policy or as required by law.

3Use of Technology, Including Artificial Intelligence

Frankly uses proprietary processes and third-party technology, including artificial intelligence (“AI”) tools, to assist in the delivery of the Services — for example, in transaction categorization, financial reporting, and drafting of documents and communications.

Where AI tools are used:

  • (a) they are engaged under commercial agreements that contractually prohibit the provider from using Client Information to train its models;
  • (b) Client Information processed through such tools remains subject to the confidentiality obligations in Section 2; and
  • (c) all client deliverables — including financial reports, filings, and recommendations — are reviewed and approved by a qualified member of our professional team before release. Responsibility and accountability for all work product rests with Frankly at all times.

4Third-Party Service Providers

We engage a limited number of third-party service providers to support the delivery of the Services. These providers are permitted to process Client Information only to the extent necessary to perform their functions and are bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations consistent with this Policy. The categories we use are:

  • accounting and bookkeeping software, where your books are kept;
  • email, document storage, and our client portal, where documents you send us are held;
  • cloud hosting for the password-protected financial dashboards we prepare for you;
  • payment processing for our invoices; and
  • AI infrastructure providers, subject to Section 3.

A current list of the named providers we use, and where each stores data, is available on request. We do not add a new provider that will hold Client Information unless it meets the requirements of this Section.

5QuickBooks and Connected Accounting Platforms

Some of the Services are delivered through our client portal, which can connect to your accounting platform — including QuickBooks Online, through Intuit’s authorized connection — with your consent. When you connect QuickBooks, we access only the accounting data needed to deliver the Services, such as your company information, chart of accounts, transactions, invoices and bills, and account balances.

QuickBooks data is used for one purpose: doing your books. That means bookkeeping, reconciliations, financial reporting, and tax work under your engagement. It is treated as Client Information under this Policy — we do not sell it, we do not share it with any third party except as described in this Policy or as required by law, and we never use it for advertising or any purpose unrelated to your engagement.

6Disconnecting QuickBooks

You can disconnect QuickBooks from the Services at any time: from Intuit’s My Apps page in your QuickBooks account (choose Disconnect), or by emailing us at hello@franklyfinancial.co and asking us to disconnect it for you.

Once disconnected, we stop reading data from your QuickBooks account immediately. Data we already obtained through the connection is kept only for as long as our professional and legal record-keeping obligations require (see the Retention section below), after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

7Where Your Information Is Stored

Canadian privacy law does not require Client Information to be stored in Canada, but it does require that it be protected to the same standard wherever it is held. Several of our service providers, including our cloud hosting and document storage providers, store or process information on servers located in the United States. Where Client Information is processed outside of Canada, it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions, including lawful access by courts and government authorities. We take reasonable contractual and technical measures to ensure that Client Information receives a comparable level of protection wherever it is processed.

Quebec clients. Before communicating personal information outside Quebec, we assess whether it will receive adequate protection, as required by Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector.

Canadian-only storage. If your contract, regulator, or professional body requires that your information be stored only in Canada, tell us before your engagement begins and we will confirm in writing what we can accommodate.

8Security

We protect Client Information using administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity, including access controls limiting Client Information to team members working on your engagement, encryption in transit, password protection on client dashboards and portals, and secure cloud-based storage. We collect only the information we need to deliver the Services. Where your books contain information about your own customers or patients, we keep it within your books and do not reproduce it in dashboards or reports unless you ask us to.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. In the event of a breach of security safeguards creating a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected clients and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as feasible, as required by applicable law. We keep a record of every breach of security safeguards for at least 24 months.

9Retention

We retain Client Information only as long as necessary to deliver the Services, satisfy our professional and legal record-keeping obligations (including obligations under the Income Tax Act (Canada) and the Excise Tax Act, which generally require books and records to be kept for six years from the end of the tax year to which they relate), and resolve disputes. When Client Information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

10Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent to certain uses of your information (which may limit our ability to deliver the Services). Requests should be directed to our Privacy Officer using the contact details below, and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11Compliance

This Policy is intended to comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable Canadian privacy legislation, as well as the professional standards applicable to our team. Our Privacy Officer is accountable for our compliance with this Policy, including the practices of the service providers we engage.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated to active clients.
