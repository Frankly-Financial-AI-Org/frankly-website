    How we compared. Each bar is the lowest month-to-month bookkeeping price the firm publishes for a plan that covers the transaction count you picked, in Canadian dollars before tax, as shown on its own website on 18 Nov 2026. Firm names link to the page we read. We never estimate a price a firm doesn't publish; a firm without a published price at your size is left off the chart and named under it. “From” is a firm's starting rate; “~” is the firm's own estimate. Outsource Bookkeeping also publishes $585 a month for its Standard plan when billed annually. Where we show a saving against iAccounting, we add our $750-a-year corporate return to our price, because iAccounting's plans include one (with revenue over $200K our return is $1,250, and the saving at up to 150 transactions is $196 a month). What's included differs: our plans include HST/GST filing; iAccounting's plans include the year-end corporate return (T2); Outsource Bookkeeping and Ontario Tax Team include HST filing; Think Accounting's estimate covers bookkeeping plus a payroll base; Zenbooks and LiveCA aim their plans at businesses with about $1M or more in revenue, and LiveCA's include tax.

    Our tax returns and payroll are priced separately: corporate return (T2) $750 a year ($1,250 over $200K revenue), personal return with business income (T1) $375 a year, payroll $119 a month plus $12 a person, catch-up $250 a month behind. We're not the lowest on everything: at up to 40 transactions a month, iAccounting's $210 plan with its included corporate return costs less than our $179 plan plus our $750 corporate return; iAccounting also publishes lower prices for a personal return and for payroll with a few employees; TurboTax's do-it-yourself software costs less if you file yourself; and above 600 transactions a month Outsource Bookkeeping's (from $650) and Ontario Tax Team's ($800) unlimited plans start below our Scale plan ($899). TurboTax lists tax returns only (2025 tax year); H&R Block offers bookkeeping at participating offices without a published price. BDO, MNP, Grant Thornton, RSM and Baker Tilly: we found no published prices on their sites.
